## 💡 Engineering principles

> Build for the engineer who has to extend the codebase next, not just for the user who opens the app today.

- **Small surface area** — Keep public APIs focused and module responsibilities clear.
- **Explicit dependencies** — Prefer clear contracts and dependency injection over hidden side effects.
- **Performance is a feature** — Measure before optimizing; protect responsiveness and resource usage.
- **Accessibility by design** — Treat inclusive interaction as a core requirement, not a finishing task.
- **Testable boundaries** — Keep business rules independently testable and easy to reason about.
- **Evolution over cleverness** — Choose understandable solutions that can adapt as requirements change.
